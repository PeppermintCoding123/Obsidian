[# Definition:Regular Domain](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Regular_Domain)
Let M be a [topological manifold](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Topological_Manifold "Definition:Topological Manifold").

Let D⊆M be a [submanifold](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Submanifold "Definition:Submanifold").

Suppose D

is a [closed](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Closed_Set_\(Topology\) "Definition:Closed Set (Topology)"), [embedded](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Embedded_Submanifold "Definition:Embedded Submanifold"), [codimension-0](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Codimension "Definition:Codimension")
[](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Codimension "Definition:Codimension")[submanifold](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Submanifold "Definition:Submanifold") with a [boundary](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition:Boundary_\(Topology\) "Definition:Boundary (Topology)").

Then D is called the **regular domain**.
