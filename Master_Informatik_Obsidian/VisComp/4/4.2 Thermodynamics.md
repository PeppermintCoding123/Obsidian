# 2. Temperature in Rising Thermal
- warm & humid air in thermal rises, when it has lower density than surrounding air
- atmosphere has established temperature profile (dependent on weather)
- thermal changes local temperature because of expansion to lower pressure at higher altitudes

Model assumptions / simplification:
- Heat exchange of thermal with surrounding atmosphere neglected
- assume [[isentropic]] expansion

![[(10) SSCDitN]]
$\gamma_th = Y_V \gamma_V + (1-Y_V)\gamma_{air}$
---
$\hat{T}, \hat{p}$ = condition at ground
$\gamma$ = isentrophic exponent
$\gamma_{air} = 1.4$ isentrophic exponent of air
$\gamma_V = 1.33$ isentrophic exponent of water vapor
$Y_V$ = mass fraction of water in thermal

# 3. Buoyancy and Acceleration of the Thermal
- because atmosphere density & thermal density is different => vertical acceleration [[Archimedes`principle]]

$$B = g\Big(\frac{\mathcal{M}_{air}T_{th}}{\mathcal{M}_{th}T_{air}} - 1\Big)$$
pressure equilibrated between thermal and surrounding air, at every altitude

Herleitung:
$L = Vg(\rho_{air}-\rho_{th})$ = upward lift of humid thermal
$B = \frac{L}{m} = \frac{Vg(\rho_{air}-\rho_{th})}{V\rho_{th}} = g\Big(\frac{\rho_{air}}{\rho_{th}} - 1\Big)$ = Buoyant acceleration on thermal parcel with [[Newtons 2 law]]
$\rho = \frac{p}{RT} = \frac{p}{(R/M)T}$
