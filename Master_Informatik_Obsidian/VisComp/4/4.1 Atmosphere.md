# 1. Atmospheric pressure
$$p(z)= p_0 \Big (1-\frac{\Gamma_z}{T_0}\Big)^{\frac{g}{RT}}$$
Herleitung:
**hydrostatic equation**
$d_zp = -\rho g$
$d_z p=  -\frac{p \cdot M \cdot g}{RT}$(Ideal gas)
$\frac{d_z p}{p}=  -\frac{M \cdot g}{RT}$
(integrate)
$\int_0^z \frac{dp}{p} = \frac{g}{R}\int_0^z \frac{dz}{T(z)} = \frac{g}{R} \int_0^z \frac{dz}{T_0 + \Gamma_z}$
