$$\begin{matrix} 
x_1 (I ) = F_1 {x_1 (I − d_{11}  ), . . . , xv (I − d_{V 1} )} \quad I ∈ \mathcal{I}_1
\\
\vdots
\\
x_V (I ) = F_V {x_1 (I − d_{1V} ), . . . , x_v (I − d_{VV} )} \quad
I ∈ IV
\end{matrix}$$
- index set $\mathcal{I}_i \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^n$ = convex & n-dimensional
- $V$ = index variables $x_j(I), j = 1, ..., V \; I\in \mathcal{I}_j$
- If $x_j(I)$ computed from $x_i(I-d_{ij})$
	- $d_{ij}$ = constant vector
	- $\forall I \in \mathcal{I}_j$
		- if $I-d_{ij}\in \mathcal{I}_i$ : $x_j(I)$ directly depends on $x_i(I-d_{ij})$
		- if $I-d_{ij}\not \in \mathcal{I}_i$ :  $x_i(I-d_{ij})$ is input variable
