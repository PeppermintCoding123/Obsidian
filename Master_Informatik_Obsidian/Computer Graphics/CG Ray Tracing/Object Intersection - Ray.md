normal through points $a, b, c$:
$\color{Peach}  n = \frac{(b-a) \times (c-a)}{(b-a) \times (c-a)}$
plane equation - point-normal form:
$n \times (x-a) = 0$
constant-normal form:
$n \times x = s = n \times a = n\times b = n\times c$
