

$$L_{spec} = I_{in} \frac{D\cdot F\cdot G}{\pi (n\circ v)(n \circ l)}$$
#### $D$ = Distribution of normals
- probability distribution of inclination angles
#### $F$ = Fresnel Term
- reflection off specular surface
- more light reflected for grazing angles
#### $G$ = Geometry Term
- for rough surfases self-occlusion, especially for grazing angles.
