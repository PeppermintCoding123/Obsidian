Let $\mathcal{M}:= \langle\mathcal{D}_0, \mathcal{I}\rangle$ be model:
- $\mathcal{I}_\varphi(A)= T$ => A is true under $\varphi$ in $\mathcal{M}$
- $\mathcal{I}_\varphi(A)=F$ => A **falsifies** $\varphi$ in $\mathcal{M}$
- $\exists \varphi: \mathcal{I}_\varphi(A) = T$ => A **satsfiable** in $\mathcal{M}$
- $\exists \varphi:  \mathcal{I}_\varphi(A)= F$ => A is **falsifiable** in $\mathcal{M}$
- $\forall \varphi:  \mathcal{I}_\varphi(A)= F$ => A is **unsatisfiable** in $\mathcal{M}$
- $\forall \varphi: \mathcal{M} \vDash^\varphi A$ => A is **valid** in $\mathcal{M}$
