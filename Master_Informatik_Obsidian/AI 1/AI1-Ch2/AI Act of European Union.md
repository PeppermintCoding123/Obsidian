### Problem:
AI is used much but unpredictable effects. 

# Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act)
- common regulatory & legal framework for AI
- 1. Aug 2024 start, gradual implementation over 6-36 months

- not for individuals rights, but for AI systems and entities using AI in professional context
- apply extraterritoriality if users are in EU

### Exceptions:
- military
- national security
- research
- non-professional purposes

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Unacceptable-risk</span>
= banned
- manipulate human behavior
- real-time remote bio-metric identification in public places
- social scoring

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 133, 133)">High-risk</span>
= comply with security, transparency, quality obligations, conformity assessments
- expected threat to health, safety, fundamental rights
- including
	- health
	- education
	- recruitment
	- critical infrastructure management
	- law enforcement
	- judiciary

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 221, 0)">Limited-risk</span> 
= transparency obligation
- generate & manipulate images, sound, videos

## <span style="color:rgb(24, 203, 21)">Minimal-risk</span>
= not regulated
- videogames, spamfilters, ...

## <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">General-purpose</span> 
= transparency
- reduced for open source & additional for high-capacity
- foundationmodels


# <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">High-Risk Requirements</span>
**Risc management systems** 
- continually monitored & risc metigation actions
**Data & Data Governence**
- quality & bias reduction on training data transparently
**Technical documentation**
- available before AI system is on market
**Record-Keeping**
- comprehensive, exessive & accurate logs - tracibillity of operations
**Transparency & Provision of Information to Deployers**
- deployers need to know what they are getting into
**Human Oversight**
- must allow humans to make informed desicions on how to interpret results , when / how to use them, when not to use the system
**Accuracy, Robustness, Cybersecurity**
- it works as advertised
