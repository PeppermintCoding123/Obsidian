## Aperture priority mode
- photogropher set [[Aperture]]
- camera sets [[Shutter speed]]
- Consequences
	- Depth of field control
	- can require impossible [[Shutter speed]]s for (bright scenes)

## Shutter-speed priority
- photogropher sets [[Shutter speed]]
- camera deduces [[Aperture]]
- Consequences
	- motion blur control
	- can require impossible [[Aperture]](1/1000 speed for dark scenes)

## Program mode
- camera decides both [[Aperture]] & [[Shutter speed]] (18% grey)
- Consequenses
	- no control

## Manual
- User decides [[Shutter speed]] & [[Aperture]]
- Consequenses
	- Control & need to think
