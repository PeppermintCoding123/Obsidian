#Thin_Lense

$$\frac{1}{D\;'} + \frac{1}{D} = \frac{1}{f}$$

![[Drawing 2025-08-06 13.24.59.excalidraw]]
$f$ = focal length
$D$ = Distance to object
$D'$ = Distance to **focus** of that point

# proof
Similarity Triangles:
Opposite side of lenses:
$$\frac{y\;'}{y} = \frac{D\;'}{D}$$
on focal length side of lens
$$\quad \frac{y\;'}{y} = \frac{D\;'-f}{f}$$
