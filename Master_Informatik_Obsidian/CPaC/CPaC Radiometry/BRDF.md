= Bidirectional Reflectance Distribution Function
$$f_r(\omega_i \rightarrow \omega_o) = f_r(\theta_i, \phi_i, \theta_o, \phi_o) = \frac{dL_o(\omega_0)}{dE_i(\omega_i)}$$
$L_o$ = Outgoing Radiance
$\omega_o$ = Angle of outgoing Light 
$E_i$ = incoming Irradiance
$\omega_i$ = Angle of incoming light

# Properties
Energy Conservation
$$\int_\Omega f_r \cos \theta_o d\omega_o \leq 1$$
Helmholtz reciprocity
$$f_r(\omega_i \rightarrow \omega_o) = f_r(\omega_o \rightarrow \omega_i)$$
