## back-to-front composition
$$C_{dst} = \alpha_{src}C_{src} + (1-\alpha_{src})C_{dst}$$
## front-to-back composition
$$C_{dst} = \alpha_{dst}\alpha_{src}C_{src}+C_{dst}, \quad \alpha_{dst} = (1-\alpha_{src})\alpha_{dst}$$

$C_{dst}$ = Accmulated color
$C_{src}$ = Color provided by[[ transfer function]]
$\alpha_{dst}$ = Accumulated opacity
$\alpha_{src}$ = Opasity provided by [[transfer function]]
