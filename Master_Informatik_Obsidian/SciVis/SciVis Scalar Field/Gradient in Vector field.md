$grad f =\begin{pmatrix}f_x \\ f_y \\ f_z \end{pmatrix}$ = vectorfield
$grad f$ points in direction of maximal rate of increase (towards Maximum)

$|grad f|$ = max rate of increase of $f$ per unit distance

$\forall \mathrm{x}: grad f \neq 0$ => [[Isocontor]] $f(\mathrm{x})= c$
$grad f$ is normal to isocontor $\mathrm{x}_0$
