Domain = $[x_{min}], y_{min}]\times [x_{max}, y_{max}] \in \mathbb{R}^2$
Resolution = $X\times Y \in \mathbb{N}^2$
![[Pasted image 20250822221523.png]]
### Grid Spacing:
$$(\Delta _x, \Delta _y) = (\frac{x_{max} - x_{min}}{X-1}, \frac{y_{max} - y_{min}}{Y-1})$$
### Coordinate for grid index $(1, 1)$
	- $(0,0)$ immer unten links
### Coordinate
$$(x_i, y_j)= (x_{min} + 1 \cdot \Delta_x, y_{min} + 1 \cdot \Delta_y)$$
