=> Isosurface of #ScalarField 

## $p \leq p_{thresh}$ 
- Threshold on **pressure**
- local criterion
- steady in 2D, unsteady in 3D

## $|\nabla \times v| \geq \omega_{thresh}$
- threshold on **[[Vorticity]] magnitude**
- easy to implement
- false positive in shear flow

## $|(\nabla \times v)\cdot v|\geq h_{thresh}$
- threshold on **Helicity**
- fails for curved shear layers
- Vortex must alighn with flow direction
