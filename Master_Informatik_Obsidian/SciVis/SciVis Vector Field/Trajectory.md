## Prescribe Velocity
$$\frac{d\mathrm{x}(t)}{d t} = \mathrm{u}(\mathrm{x}(t))$$
Prescribe Acceleration
$$\frac{d^2\mathrm{x}(t)}{d t^2} = \mathrm{g}(\mathrm{x}(t))$$
$$\frac{d}{d t}\begin{pmatrix}\mathrm{x}(t)\\ \mathrm{v}(t)\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{v}(t)\\
\mathrm{u}(\mathrm{x}(t))\end{pmatrix}$$
