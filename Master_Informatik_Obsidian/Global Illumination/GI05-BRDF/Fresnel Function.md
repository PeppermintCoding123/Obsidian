- porition of light being reflected
s-Polarization $R_s$ - perpendicular to plane
p-Polarization $R_p$ -  parallel to plane
$$R = \frac{R_s + R_p}{2}$$
Transmission coefficiant    $T =1−R$

If light = normal incident
$$R_0 = \Big ( \frac{n_1-n_2}{n_1+n_2}\Big )^2 \quad T_0 = \frac{4n_1n_2}{(n_1+n_2)^2}$$

- also for conductors & semi-conductors with complex index of refraction
