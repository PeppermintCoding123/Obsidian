# Monte-Carlo Integration
to compute use approx:
$$\int_D f(x)dx \approx \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i = 1}^n \frac{f(x_i)}{p(x_i)}$$
# Apply
$$L(x, \omega) \approx (\frac{1}{n}\sum \frac{T(x, y_i)}{p(y_i)}\Big[ \mu_a(x)L_e(x, \omega) + \mu_s(x)L_s(x, \omega)\Big]) + \frac{T(x,z)}{P(z)} L_o(z,\omega)$$
$p(y)$ probability dencity of distance y
$P(z)$ probability of exceeding distance z = exit point of volume

Big sum = path scatters in medioum
last + part = path leaves medium
