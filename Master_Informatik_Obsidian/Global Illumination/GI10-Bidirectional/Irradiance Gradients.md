#### Translational gradient $\nabla_t E$
- how fast gradient changes if surface moves?
	- depends on variance of [[incident light]]
	- depends on closeness of environment
#### Rotational Gradient $\nabla _r E$
- irradiance changes with normal of chache 

#### Taylor-Extrapolation:
$$E(x,n) = E_i + \nabla_tE(x-x_i)+ \nabla_rE(n \times n_i)$$
$x$ position
$n$ normal
$E_i$ irradiance
$n\times n_i$ approx near-by hemisphere
