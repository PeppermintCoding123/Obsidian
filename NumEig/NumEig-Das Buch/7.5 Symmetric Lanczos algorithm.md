#LanczosAlgorithm 

$A^* = A\in \mathbb{F}^{\;n \times \; n}$ self-adjoint = $H_m = \mathcal{Q}_m^* A \mathcal{Q}_m$ is self-adjoint:
$H_m = \begin{bmatrix}h_{11} & \dots & \dots & h_{1n} \\ h_{21} & h_{22} & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & \ddots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \vdots & \ddots & h_{m,m-1} & h_{mm} \end{bmatrix}$ = $\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_1 & \beta_1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ \beta_1 & \alpha_2 & \beta_2 & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & \beta_2 & \ddots & \ddots & 0 \\ \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & \beta_{m-1} \\ 0 & \dots & 0 & \beta_{m-1} & \alpha_m \end{bmatrix}$ 
$\alpha_k = h_{kk}\in \mathbb{R}$ 
$\beta_{k-1} := h_{k,k-1} = ||\tilde{q}_k||\in\mathbb{R}$
$H_m$ is tridiagonal & real (even if $A$ is complex.)
for any initial vector $x\in\mathbb{F}^{\; n}\backslash \{0\}$
