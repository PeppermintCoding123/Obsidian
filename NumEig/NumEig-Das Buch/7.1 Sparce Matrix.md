### Store Matrix values differently:
represented as values (in l), rows (in r) $\rightarrow$ and columns (in c)  $\downarrow$

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 & 5 \end{bmatrix}
\;
=>
\;
\mathbf{l}=\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \\ 4 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}
, 
\:
\mathbf{r}=\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 1 \\ 2 \\ 4 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}
,
\:
\mathbf{c}=\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}
$$

### Matrix-Vector multiplication is far more efficient
normal: $n*n=n^2$ multiplications and $n*(n-1)=n^2-n$ additions => $2n^2-n$ operations.
Depending on Matrix, it can be much faster (in $O(n)$)

Sometimes rather store $x$ and $y$ s.t. $y=Ax$ 
=> A is not explicitly given
