[[Ritz pair]] 

How to compute Ritz values and Ritz vectors if $\mathcal{X}$ is given
#### Theorem 7.3
"Let $A\in \mathbb{F}^{\:n\times n}$ and let $\mathcal{X} \subseteq \mathbb{F}^{\,n}$ be as subspace.
Let $Q \in \mathbb{F}^{\:n\times n}$ be isometric matrix 
=> $\mathcal{X} = \mathcal{R}(Q)$, i.e. the columns of $Q$ form an orthonormal basis of $\mathcal{X}$ 
=> these 2 Conditions are equivalent for $x = Qy \in \mathcal{X}$, where $y \in \mathbb{F}^{\:m}$

1. $(\mu, Qy) \in \mathbb{F} \times \mathcal{X}$ is a Ritz pair of A with respect to $\mathcal{X}$
2. $(\mu, Qy) \in \mathbb{F} \times \mathbb{F}^{m}$ is an eigenpair of the $m \times m$ matrix $Q * AQ$ "
